# Non-claims

What VOLE-GFX does **not** claim, regardless of how its README or reports are
worded:

1. No claim that procedural representation beats information theory, or that
   arbitrary imagery/video has a short deterministic cause.
2. No universal performance claim of any backend over any other; backend
   comparisons are scoped to corpus × hardware × profile × request shape and
   backed by receipts.
3. No CUDA result may be reported as verified on a host without a genuine
   NVIDIA device and driver; the status is `not evaluated on this host`.
4. No exact-language may be used for a quality-bounded result.
5. No claim of "direct display" / "no full frame" unless the traced path
   actually avoids a complete coherent raster before the compatibility
   boundary.
6. No storage-only win may be described as procedural *computing*; the
   no-mandatory-re-baking court decides that classification.
7. No skipped test, unsupported hardware path, or placeholder may be
   converted into a pass.
8. No claim of inverse compilation recovering the "true semantics" of an
   asset's author; the compiler only seeks a bounded explanation that wins
   under the declared cost model.
9. No claim that a generator "explains" pixels unless exact reconstruction
   follows from counted deterministic state (pathological-overfit guard).

The claims ledger (`evidence/claims.json`) is the machine-readable source of
truth; any prose that contradicts it is a bug.
